About SkinLytics

SkinLytics is an AI-powered skincare assistant that helps users analyze their skin conditions and receive personalized skincare recommendations.

Using intelligent image analysis, it detects issues like acne, dryness, and oiliness, and suggests the best skincare products and routines.

Our goal is to make skincare simple, smart, and accessible for everyone.
